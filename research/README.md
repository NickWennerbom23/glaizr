## How transcripts changed the model

The biggest single shift was **Ventora's $100M glass slice** (Greg, verbatim 20 May). Perplexity treated Stegbar at A$157M whole-brand and Regency at A$30M separately — but they've been consolidated under Ventora Group with windows/doors as separate brands (A&L, Trend, Corinthian, Breezway). The glass slice is A$100M, not the A$30-40M Perplexity haircut implied.

Second biggest: **Crystal at A$17-22M**, not A$5-8M. Justin's 5,000 homes × A$3-4k average ticket (sole-supply key-account business doing showers + mirrors + splashbacks + wardrobes) cleanly derives a much higher revenue than any desk-research source.

Third: **ATOG at A$19-27M reconciled** (A$25-35M transcript-only). Perplexity mis-mapped ATOG to "Touch of Glass" at A$2.5M — wrong company. Ty said 200+ staff, 12 VIC measurers, and biggest in VIC — implies A$25-35M minimum.
