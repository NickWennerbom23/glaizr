## Section 6 — United Kingdom (GBP → AUD) — REVISED 2026-05-28 after Nick challenge

**Original memo claim:** "The UK does not have direct ICP equivalents." **That was overstated.** Nick correctly pushed back by naming CreoGlass, MDM Glass, and Silver Saints as London supply-and-install operators. After Companies House verification, the corrected picture is:

**The UK DOES have a strict-ICP integrated installer category — but it's three structural differences from Australia:**

1. **No UK glass installer exceeds ~£6M revenue.** There is no UK Stegbar (A$100M), no UK ATOG (A$28-38M), no UK Crystal (A$17-22M). The market caps out an order of magnitude smaller per operator.
2. **Glass and wardrobes are split into separate verticals.** Glass installers (showers + mirrors + splashbacks + balustrades) do NOT do wardrobes. Wardrobe specialists (Hammonds £98M, Sharps £135M, Sliderobes, Spaceslide) do NOT do glass. The AU pattern of one company doing both is an Australian quirk.
3. **Concentration is in London, not nationally distributed.** The wealth, density, and premium-residential glass demand sits in London — outside London, the category is small local glaziers.
