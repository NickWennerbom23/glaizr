## Comparison to Deep-Research-Report

### What the report got right
- **Macro scale:** A$1.4bn–A$1.9bn for shower screens + wardrobes + splashbacks is plausible (your segmented data: A$780M + A$625M + A$185M = A$1.59bn — very close).

- **Exclusivity is expensive:** Report's conclusion holds, but the actual cost to Glaizr is A$370K–A$1M, not A$420m–A$550M.

### What the report missed
- **Operator counts:** Report never asked "how many companies is the A$1.4bn spread across?" Your data: 250–300 in AU, 70–90 in VIC.
- **SOM vs TAM confusion:** Report conflated end-market spend with software SOM. Your model is clearer: A$1.4M cumulative 3-year software revenue (AU), not A$370K–A$1M annual.
- **Segment breakdown:** Report lumped shower screens + wardrobes + splashbacks together. Your data separates them and shows wardrobe internals dominate (50% of global TAM).
