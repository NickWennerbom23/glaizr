**Implication:** Option A exclusivity premium needs to clear A$2.4M to be strategically neutral. Per Courtney, ATOG's current offer is "much higher than A$10K" — likely A$50k-A$150k. That's **15-50× below neutral**.

ATOG almost certainly cannot fund A$2.4M in cash. **Option A is effectively off the table at any realistic price point.**

## Option B — Equity-with-uplift

Ty + Ryan each take 5% of Glaizr (per Courtney's cap table: Nick 40 / CS 40 / Ty 5 / Ryan 5 / 10 unallocated).

**Rough valuation framing:**
- 2% of AU SAM (A$194-428M) × 30% conversion ≈ A$1.2-2.6M ARR floor

**Implication:** Option B aligns ATOG's upside with the A$500M national TAM, not just the A$156M VIC slice. Ty and Ryan are thinking "do we want 5% of an A$500M-AU + A$280-470M-UK + A$160-225M-CA + A$140-200M-TX play?" — not "should we buy exclusivity?"
